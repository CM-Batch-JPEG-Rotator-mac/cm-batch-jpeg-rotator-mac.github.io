FAQ

Why do my photos rotate wrong on non-Apple devices?

JPEGs store orientation in EXIF headers. Apple reads this; most others don't. CM Batch JPEG Rotator physically rotates the image and updates headers.

Does this affect image quality?

Lossless mode preserves every pixel. Standard mode lets you set JPEG quality—no surprises.

Can I process entire folders?

Yes. Drag folders for recursive batch processing. Handles thousands of files efficiently.
